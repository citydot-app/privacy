citydot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · App: citydot (package com.citydot.app)

citydot helps you discover places to visit in India. We built it so that your personal data stays on your phone. We have no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of our own.

Location

With your permission, citydot uses your device's precise or approximate location only while the app is open, to show places near you, distances and travel times, and to plan your day from where you are. Location is never collected in the background. You can turn it off any time in the app (Settings → Location permission) or in Android settings; the app still works by choosing a city.

What leaves your phone

To find places, the app sends the coordinates of the area you are viewing — never your name, contacts or any device ID — to these public services:

  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia – famous places and photos (privacy policy)
  • Photon by Komoot, using OpenStreetMap data – nearby places and addresses (privacy policy)
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim – nearby hospitals, pharmacies and other essentials, addresses (privacy policy)
  • OpenStreetMap API – entry fee and opening hours of a place you open (privacy policy)

When you tap directions or a map link, Google Maps opens and Google's privacy policy applies. When you tap a phone number or WhatsApp, your dialer or WhatsApp opens.

What is stored on your phone

Your last known location, your My Day plan (stops, date, start time, start point), your theme choice and cached place lists/photos are saved only on your device, so the app opens fast. Nothing is synced to or backed up by us.

Your choices & deleting data

In the app, Settings → Clear cached place data removes cached lists, and Settings → Delete all my data removes everything citydot stored. Uninstalling the app also removes all of it. Because we keep no data on servers, there is nothing else to delete.

Sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, and we do not use any data for advertising.

Children

citydot is a general travel guide and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app with a new date.
